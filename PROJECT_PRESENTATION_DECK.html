Full-Stack SaaS Platform

SocialPilot: Enterprise Social Media & Campaign Hub

A high-performance social scheduling, AI-assisted content creation, and cross-channel campaign management platform with complete 4-role multi-tenant governance.

🛡️ Administrator 💼 Business User 📊 Marketing Team ✏️ Content Creator
Role 1

Administrator

User provisioning, content moderation flags, live infrastructure health telemetry, and immutable security audit logs.

Role 2

Business User

OAuth channel links, subscription tier manager, resource quota meters, payment wallet, and printable tax receipts.

Role 3

Marketing Team

Campaign strategy with dynamic timeline auto-evaluation, engagement heatmaps, audience metrics, and executive reports.

Role 4

Content Creator

Free AI caption studio, high-res graphic generator, multi-channel mockup previews, drafts vault, and publishing queue.

🛡️ 1. Administrator Features (Governance & Security)

User Governance & Provisioning (/admin/users) Security

Why & How: Full tenant oversight. Admins search, filter by role, instantly promote/demote user permissions, and suspend accounts with real-time database synchronization.

Content Moderation & Policy Compliance (/admin/content) Compliance

Why & How: Protects brand integrity. Inspects cross-platform post copies, target networks, and media attachments with 1-click Approve or Flag/Reject actions.

System Health & Telemetry Console (/admin/system-health) DevOps

Why & How: Real-time monitoring of backend API latency, database response times, server memory consumption, active worker threads, and an audit events log.

💼 2. Business User Features (Billing & Subscription Hub)

Enterprise Billing & Quotas Hub (/billing) Exclusively Business

Why & How: Strict RBAC protection keeps financial controls limited to business owners. Includes live resource quota gauges, tier plan comparisons (Starter, Growth, Scale, Enterprise), monthly/annual toggle (-20% discount), modular add-on boosters, credit card wallet, and printable tax receipts.

OAuth Social Channels Integration (/social-accounts) Channels

Why & How: Central hub to link Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and Pinterest accounts with live connection indicators and follower counters.

📊 3. Marketing Team Features (Campaigns & Heatmaps)

Campaign Strategy & Auto-Timeline Engine (/campaigns) Timeline Engine

Why & How: Automatically synchronizes campaign statuses. Evaluates event dates to mark campaigns as Active (ongoing), Upcoming (future), or Completed (concluded). Features live creation preview cards and filter tabs with dynamic counts.

BI Analytics & 24x7 Best-Time-To-Post Heatmap (/analytics) Analytics

Why & How: Recharts-powered graphs comparing reach, impressions, audience engagement, and an interactive 24x7 hour-by-day activity heatmap.

✏️ 4. Content Creator Features (AI Studio & Mockups)

Free AI Text & Graphic Generation Studio (/posts/create) AI Studio

Why & How: Embedded Pollinations AI generates viral captions, tone adjustments, hashtags, and high-resolution visuals across multiple aspect ratios without requiring paid API keys.

Interactive Drag-and-Drop Calendar & Queue (/calendar, /queue) Scheduling

Why & How: Visual monthly planner allowing creators to drag posts to reschedule dates, with a chronological publishing queue and live mockups.
